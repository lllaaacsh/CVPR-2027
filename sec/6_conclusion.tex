\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\method uses supervision from the action decoder to allocate spatiotemporal resolution in WAMs.
Its router guides spatial merging and temporal aggregation, while velocity distillation adapts the decoder to compressed context with the video backbone frozen.
Experiments on two WAM backbones show lower inference cost, including a $1.59\times$ speedup on mimic-video at 20\% future context.
On six real-robot tasks, \method achieves 77.8\% success with a $1.84\times$ speedup over Native.
Real-robot evaluation remains limited to one platform; extending the approach to longer-horizon tasks and additional platforms is future work.
